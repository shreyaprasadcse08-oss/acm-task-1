# Insight Architect - Exploratory Data Analysis & Visualizations

## Overview
This directory contains an exploratory data analysis (EDA) of student performance across math, reading, and writing exams, using five distinct visualizations to uncover trends and relationships.

---

## Key Findings & Insights

1. **High Score Correlation:** Reading and writing scores show a very strong positive correlation ($\approx 0.95$), indicating students who perform well in reading almost always score high in writing.
2. **Impact of Test Preparation:** Students who completed a test preparation course scored significantly higher on average across all three subjects compared to those who did not.
3. **Parental Education Influence:** Students whose parents hold master's or bachelor's degrees consistently achieve higher median test scores.
4. **Lunch Program Trends:** Students receiving standard lunch consistently outperform those on free/reduced lunch, highlighting socio-economic influences on academic outcomes.
5. **Score Distributions:** Math scores exhibit a wider spread and lower overall mean compared to reading and writing scores.
6. **Gender Performance Variations:** Female students outperform male students in reading and writing, whereas male students achieve slightly higher average scores in mathematics.
